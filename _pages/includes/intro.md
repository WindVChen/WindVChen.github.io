More recently, from Jan - Jul, 2026, I was a research intern at [Snap Research](https://research.snap.com/) working on 4D generation and non-rigid registration with [Chaoyang Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=I-xDKHEAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Rameen Abdal](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kEQimk0AAAAJ&hl=en). My current research interests lie in 4D reconstruction and generation, AISafety, image synthesis, and remote sensing image processing.
